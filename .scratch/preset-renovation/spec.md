# Factory preset renovation

Requested 2026-10-19: investigate the installed Serum 2 preset construction and
review/reimplement the entire Borato Wave factory bank with original sound design.

## Acceptance

- Bind evidence to the supplied installed binary; distinguish observed schema from
  inferred design choices and unknown DSP/sound-design details.
- Review every factory recipe, retaining names/order and full-state persistence.
- Correct performance metadata, useful macros, articulation and modulation choices.
- Validate preset identity, state/file round trips, finite output, headroom, tails,
  sample rates, velocity and macro extremes with bounded rendered scenarios.
- Produce the full portable bank and audio previews for listening.
- Report sonic equivalence as unverified unless controlled reference audio supports it.

## Compatibility

Existing saved states contain parameter values and resources. Revised factory recipes
apply when explicitly selected; old states may no longer match a factory name.
No Serum code, waveform or factory preset is included in the product.
