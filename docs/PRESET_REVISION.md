# Factory preset revision - 2026-10-19

All 65 factory entries were reviewed: 64 musical patches and the Init template.
Names and factory order remain stable. Recipes are original Borato sound design;
no Serum preset, waveform or implementation was copied. Equal sound or subjective
quality to Serum has not been established by these edits.

Musical patches have explicit velocity-dependent timbre, and the early collection
now has four useful macros. Mono-tagged patches select actual mono voice mode with
glide. The following table records design intent; rendered validation and any later
level calibration are recorded in [PRESET_RESEARCH.md](PRESET_RESEARCH.md).

Saved sessions and exported presets retain their stored parameters, curves and
resources. Because factory matching compares content, an older saved recipe may no
longer match the revised factory entry even though its sound data remains intact.
Selecting that factory entry explicitly loads its revised recipe.
